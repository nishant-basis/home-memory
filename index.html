OUR STORY
CHAPTER I

Before Hello

Before there was a story, there was simply a girl I noticed.

And somehow, she felt familiar.

BEFORE HELLO
✦
The first time I saw you.

The First Look

I had just walked into the venue. Most of the faces around me were familiar — common friends, people from the team, the usual crowd.

I did what I normally do. Started pulling everyone's leg. My sense of humour was doing its usual job. 😄

And then, somewhere in the middle of all those familiar faces, I noticed you.

A beautiful face.
Those big, bright eyes.
And strangely, there was something nostalgic about it.

I remember thinking:

"I know her from somewhere."

The funny part?
I didn't.
Or at least, I couldn't remember from where.

Your face reminded me of someone I knew, but my brain had conveniently decided not to tell me who.

Maybe it wasn't even the face. Maybe it was the way you carried yourself. That quiet grace with which you seemed to pull everything off.

Whatever it was, for some reason, you didn't feel completely new. You felt familiar.

And I had absolutely no idea that this random feeling was going to become the beginning of everything.

I should probably have just said hello. ♡

THE QUESTION
✦

The Very Important Question

So, naturally, the next step was to find out who you actually were.

A normal person might have walked up to you and simply asked.

I decided to demonstrate my superior social skills.

I turned to your friend and asked:

"Which team is she from?"

The small detail I had somehow overlooked was that you were standing right there.

So yes — instead of talking to you, I interviewed your friend about you while you listened.

In hindsight, it was probably the most unnecessarily complicated way of saying:

"Hi. I'm interested in knowing you."

Subtle? Not really.
Effective? Somehow. ♡

7 MARCH
♡

The First Compliment

Drunken Botanist · CyberHub · Gurgaon

Somewhere between the party and gathering enough courage, I told her:

"You are looking beautiful."

She said:

"What?"

And for approximately three seconds, I was convinced I had completely fucked it up.

But somehow I gathered the courage and repeated myself.

THE FIRST COFFEE
☕

A Very Carefully Designed Invitation

I wanted to ask her for coffee.

But asking directly felt far too dangerous. What if she said no?

So I came up with a brilliant plan.

"I'm going to get coffee for myself. If you want to come, come. Otherwise just tell me what you want, I'll take it for you."

Basically, I had already prepared an escape route in case of rejection.

And that same day came our first coffee.

Theobroma

Hot coffee. No sugar. Something she never even liked.

Funny how coffee eventually became one of our things.

THE DIGITS
✦

When I Already Had Her Number

There was also the small matter of getting her number.

Except I already had it.

Yet somehow I still managed to ask her:

"Do you have those things that people call digits... that you dial on your phone to talk to someone?"

Looking back, I have no explanation.

Apparently flirting had temporarily affected my vocabulary.

THE CHASE
♡

She Ignored Me

She ignored me for weeks.

I would message her on Teams. Being the senior, she had to reply.

But she somehow mastered the art of replying while remaining completely formal.

I, meanwhile, had entered my flirtatious spree.

Indirect hints. Random messages. Terrible shayari.

"Mein badal badal ke badla hun."

She never replied.

I continued anyway.
