Before Hello

Before there was a story, there was simply a girl I noticed.

And somehow, she felt familiar.

BEFORE HELLO
✦
The first time I saw you.

The First Look

I had just walked into the venue. Most of the faces around me were familiar — common friends, people from the team, the usual crowd.

I did what I normally do. Started pulling everyone's leg. My sense of humour was doing its usual job. 😄

And then, somewhere in the middle of all those familiar faces, I noticed you.

A beautiful face.
Those big, bright eyes.
And strangely, there was something nostalgic about it.

I remember thinking:

"I know her from somewhere."

The funny part?
I didn't.
Or at least, I couldn't remember from where.

Your face reminded me of someone I knew, but my brain had conveniently decided not to tell me who.

Maybe it wasn't even the face. Maybe it was the way you carried yourself. That quiet grace with which you seemed to pull everything off.

Whatever it was, for some reason, you didn't feel completely new. You felt familiar.

And I had absolutely no idea that this random feeling was going to become the beginning of everything.

I should probably have just said hello. ♡

THE QUESTION
✦

The Very Important Question

By this point, one thing had become clear.

I wanted to know who you were.

A sensible person would have walked over, introduced himself, and simply asked.

Apparently, sensible was not the route I had chosen that evening.

So I asked your friend which team you were from.

Nothing particularly strange about that... except for one tiny detail.

You were standing right there.

Yes. I was asking someone else about you while you were close enough to hear the whole thing.

Looking back, I suppose that was my very first attempt at finding a way into your story without actually having the courage to start it directly.

Smooth move, Nishant. ♡

7 MARCH
♡
DRUNKEN BOTANIST · CYBERRHUB · GURGAON

The First Compliment

Somewhere between the party and gathering enough courage, I finally told you:

ME

"You are looking beautiful."

YOU

"What?"

And suddenly, the confidence I had managed to collect disappeared completely.

Because apparently, saying something nice is one thing. Surviving the moment after saying it is another.

I had no clever follow-up. No perfectly timed second line. Just the very real feeling that I had probably made things awkward.

And that was my first compliment to you.
I still remember the "What?" ♡

THE FIRST COFFEE
☕

A Very Carefully Designed Invitation

I wanted to ask her for coffee.

But asking directly felt far too dangerous. What if she said no?

So I came up with a brilliant plan.

"I'm going to get coffee for myself. If you want to come, come. Otherwise just tell me what you want, I'll take it for you."

Basically, I had already prepared an escape route in case of rejection.

And that same day came our first coffee.

Theobroma

Hot coffee. No sugar. Something she never even liked.

Funny how coffee eventually became one of our things.

THE DIGITS
✦

When I Already Had Her Number

There was also the small matter of getting her number.

Except I already had it.

Yet somehow I still managed to ask her:

"Do you have those things that people call digits... that you dial on your phone to talk to someone?"

Looking back, I have no explanation.

Apparently flirting had temporarily affected my vocabulary.

THE CHASE
♡

She Ignored Me

She ignored me for weeks.

I would message her on Teams. Being the senior, she had to reply.

But she somehow mastered the art of replying while remaining completely formal.

I, meanwhile, had entered my flirtatious spree.

Indirect hints. Random messages. Terrible shayari.

"Mein badal badal ke badla hun."

She never replied.

I continued anyway.

When I Miss You

Not every thought needs a particular date, occasion or reason. Some simply arrive — and deserve somewhere to stay.

When I miss you, the first thing that comes to mind isn't some big, cinematic memory.

It's the little things.

Your smile in the middle of an ordinary moment.
That specific tone in your voice when you're teasing me.
Those lighthearted taunts that somehow always catch me off guard.

It's realizing how strangely quiet a routine day can feel without the spontaneous, silly fun we usually share.

The inside jokes.
The banter over absolutely nothing.
The effortless comfort of simply having you around.

I think that's what missing you really starts with.

Not one particular moment.

Just all those tiny things that became so familiar that I stopped noticing how much they meant.

Because somewhere along the way, those little things became home.

You will probably never fully understand what you mean to me — or all the ways you've changed me as a human.

Because when I'm with you, I feel like the real me.

The version without the filters.
The version that doesn't have to be serious all the time.
The version whose inner child somehow gets permission to come out and play.

Sometimes I honestly feel like I'm the girlfriend in this relationship. 😂

The way you handle my tantrums.
The way you somehow always make me feel special.
The way you feed me with your own hands.

The little things that probably don't even register as big moments to you.

Like sitting beside each other at a restaurant instead of across the table — just so you can lean over my shoulder.

Or the way you keep updating me about the smallest, most random things happening in your day.

None of these things would make sense as some grand romantic gesture.

But that's exactly the point.

Somewhere between the teasing, the tantrums, the little updates, the food you make me eat, and the shoulder you lean on...

I became a version of myself that I didn't know was still there.

And maybe that's one of the biggest things you've given me.

You made me feel completely comfortable being me.

You don't always say that you care about me.

You just... do things.

You make me eat when I would happily keep talking and forget about food.

You notice the little things.

You keep updating me about the smallest things happening around you — something from work, something your friend said, some random story you just discovered — because somehow I always become the person you want to tell first.

You even change what you eat sometimes because I don't eat spicy food.

And then, once in a while, you look at something with that "tadak bhadak chahiye" expression and go back to your Kurkure anyway. 😂

You scold me when I waste money.

You find coupons when we go out and somehow turn my so-called "high maintenance girl" into the person making sure we don't pay more than we need to.

My ambitions.
My decisions.
The things you think I should be doing better.

Sometimes it feels less like you're giving advice and more like you're my very own personal management department. 😂

But underneath all of that is something I've come to understand.

You care deeply.

About me.
About your family.
About the people you love.
About the things that matter to you.

You may have your tantrums.
You may get upset when you don't feel prioritized.
You may sometimes feel like nobody understands you.

But give you a little love and attention... and somehow you multiply it and give it all back.

That's the thing about you.

You always say:

"I have loved you the most."

And you know what?

I believe you.

Because honestly, no one has ever loved me the way you do.

You were also the one who taught me something I don't think I'll forget:

There is no "love you." There is only "I love you."

Because love isn't something that simply exists between two people. It's something one person chooses to give to the other.

And you're also the one who keeps reminding me that when it's us, there isn't always a right person and a wrong person.

There is just us.

I don't think you realise how deeply this particular thought has stayed with me.

When you say:

"Tum sahi galat karte reh jaoge... aur sab khatm ho jayega."

It hits me every single time.

Because you're right.

I can keep proving that I'm right.
You can keep proving that you're right.

We can keep counting who said what, who did what, who was wrong and who was justified.

And somewhere in all that...

sab khatm ho jayega.

Sometimes the relationship matters more than winning the argument.

Sometimes saying "I'm right" isn't worth losing "us."

And maybe that's another way you love me.

You don't just love the person I am.
You keep reminding me to become a better person for the person I love.

So yes...

You always say you've loved me the most.

I accept.

No one has ever loved me quite the way you do.

And maybe that's why I've learned to notice the little things too.

The food you make me eat.
The random updates.
The scolding.
The coupons.
The "Kya hai aapko?" when I start pulling your leg.

All those tiny things that, individually, don't look like much.

But put them together...

that's how you love.

There are so many things about you that make absolutely no sense to me.

And somehow, I love every single one of them.

The way you can turn the smallest thing into a full discussion.

Kisi ki ek Instagram pic se poori story bana leti ho.

And somehow, we have probably fixed the relationships of half the people on Instagram without them even knowing about it.

Breakup.
Patch-up.
Who is cheating.
Who should leave whom.

Pata nahi kitne logon ki love life humne hawa mein hi solve kar di hai. 😂

And then there are our random stories.

One minute we're talking about something completely normal, and suddenly we're discussing something so random that I genuinely don't remember how we got there.

And if you remember...

we have almost travelled half the world together without actually going anywhere.

We've planned countries.
Checked flights.
Compared hotels.
Looked at places to visit.
Made itineraries.
Discussed what we would eat.

And then knowingly closed everything because abhi koi plan nahi hai. 😂

But somehow, the planning itself becomes a trip.

Then there is the way you choose gifts for me.

You seriously surprise me.

Two months before someone's birthday, you will start:

"Birthday aa raha hai..."

And then the investigation begins.

Because you remember things I said months ago that I don't even remember saying.

You somehow manage to get things out of me without me realising that I'm giving you the answer.

You figure out what I want before I even know I'm going to get it.

Whether it's the Adidas shoes.
The Armani bag.
Or something completely random that I mentioned once and forgot about five minutes later.

And then comes the actual operation.

Finding it.
Getting it.
Hiding it.
Getting it packed.
Making sure I don't find out.

Seriously... WTF.

Who pays this much attention to such tiny things?

And then there were the tulips.

I had casually told you that roses are common and that I like tulips.

That's it.
Just something I said.

And you somehow got tulips arranged all the way from Bangalore.

I was literally crying when I saw them.

Because it wasn't really about the flowers.

It was about the fact that you remembered.

And then I said I was fed up with Nike...

and you got me Superstars. 😂

Like seriously...

who cares about these minute things?

Apparently, you do.

And then, as if all of this wasn't enough... there is our completely unnecessary talent of bitching about random people.

Someone's face we don't like.
Someone's dressing.
Someone's attitude.
Someone's harkatein.

Someone says something weird.
Someone does something stupid.

And somehow...

everyone becomes a discussion topic.

We can find a topic in literally anyone. 😂

And honestly?

I think that's one of my favourite things about us.

Because all these things are so ridiculously ordinary.

The random Instagram investigation.
The imaginary world tours.
The flights and hotels we have no plans of booking.
The gifts you've secretly researched for months.
The flowers you remembered.
The shoes you somehow figured out I wanted.
The completely unnecessary analysis of strangers.

None of these things, individually, should mean this much.

But they do.

Because they're your little things.

And somewhere along the way, they became our little things.

And I don't think you realise how much I love that.
